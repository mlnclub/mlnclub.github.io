### Interactive: speculative decoding, one round at a time

A draft model guesses three tokens ahead, one pass each; the target model scores
all of them in a single parallel pass; then each guess is accepted or rejected in
turn, and the first rejection is resampled from the corrected distribution
norm(max(0, p − q)). Toy probabilities, three rounds. Visualization generated by Claude.

### Interactive: where p comes from

The target model as a decoder-only transformer, one column per token: embedding,
two blocks, final norm, unembedding, logits, softmax. Three plain passes produce
one token each from the last column's logits; then the same three tokens go in
as draft guesses and the same distributions come out of a single pass. Same toy
numbers as above. Visualization generated by Claude.
