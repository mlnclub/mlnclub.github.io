# Content rules — no invented text, exact attribution

Chris does **not** want AI-invented or paraphrased user-facing copy anywhere on this
site. This is the strongest editorial rule in the repo. On this site that mostly means
one thing: **week titles, summaries and descriptions come verbatim from the Luma
event** — never write a blurb for a paper.

- **Pull wording verbatim from the real source** (Luma event descriptions, actual
  paper titles, his own notebook text) rather than writing blurbs or summaries. If
  you must trim, trim to real phrases — don't reword.
- **Get attributions exactly right.** Precedent from this site: the week 4
  systolic-array viz was "generated by Claude," NOT built with chatjimmy.ai —
  chatjimmy.ai is *Taalas's* demo.
- **No filler or placeholder prose.** Sections stay blank until real content exists
  ("notes & resources" placeholders were stripped on request).
- **Don't reproduce other people's writing at length** (e.g. Neel Nanda's guide, the
  Socratica toolbox). Short attributed quotes + links only; this was asked and
  declined more than once — the answer stands unless the authors grant permission.
- He iterates with precise small corrections — keep edits tight and literal.
